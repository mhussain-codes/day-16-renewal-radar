# Renewal Radar — Day 16

Renewal Radar brings everyday subscriptions and recurring charges into one simple dashboard. Add a service, price, billing frequency, and next renewal date to see upcoming charges and what your plans cost per month and year.

**Live demo:** https://mhussain-codes.github.io/day-16-renewal-radar/

## Features

- Track services with price, currency, category, billing cycle, and next renewal.
- See monthly equivalents, a yearly estimate, average monthly cost, and renewals due in the next 30 days.
- Search and filter your list; entries are sorted by renewal date.
- Add, edit, or remove subscriptions.
- Export or import a JSON backup.
- Saves on this device in your browser. No account, server, analytics, or third-party libraries.

## Use

1. Open the [live demo](https://mhussain-codes.github.io/day-16-renewal-radar/).
2. Select **Add subscription** and enter the plan details.
3. Review the next renewal and monthly estimate.
4. Use the download and upload icons to back up or restore your list.

Weekly prices are converted using 52 weeks per year. Other billing cycles are divided by their number of months. Totals use the currency of the first listed subscription. Mixed currencies are not converted to a common currency; inspect individual plan amounts separately. Estimates exclude taxes, discounts, and plan changes.

## Privacy

Details are stored in local storage in this browser on this device. They are not sent to a server. Clearing browser site data removes the list, so export a backup if you need to move it.

## Run locally

Open index.html in a modern browser. No build step, connection, or third-party dependencies are required.
